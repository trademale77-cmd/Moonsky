Terms & Conditions / Support
Order Placement & Pricing

All orders placed on this site are requests to buy — they are confirmed only after Moon Sky contacts you on WhatsApp or phone to verify product availability, price, and delivery.

Product photos are for reference. Actual colour, packaging, or minor design may vary slightly from the photo. Prices shown are current at the time of viewing and can change without prior notice if not yet confirmed.

Payment (Cash on Delivery / UPI)
  • Cash on Delivery: pay the exact amount in cash to the delivery person at the time of delivery.
  • UPI: pay the full amount to the UPI ID shown at checkout, then enter your transaction ID. Your order is confirmed only after the shop verifies this payment.
  • If a UPI payment is made but no transaction ID is shared, the shop cannot confirm or process the order.
Cancellation Policy
  • An order can be cancelled by the customer only while its status is "Placed" or "Confirmed", using the Cancel button under My Orders.
  • Once an order status changes to "Shipped", it cannot be cancelled from the site — contact the shop directly on WhatsApp and refund/return will depend on the situation.
  • If paid via UPI and the order is cancelled before shipping, the amount will be refunded to the same UPI ID within a reasonable time after the shop confirms the cancellation.
  • Repeated order-and-cancel behaviour without genuine reason may lead to the shop declining future orders from that number.
Delivery

Delivery is currently available only within Delhi. Delivery timelines given by the shop are estimates and may be delayed due to traffic, weather, stock, or other genuine reasons — the shop will inform you if there is a delay.

Returns, Exchange & Damaged Items
  • If you receive a wrong, damaged, or defective item, message the shop on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with a photo/video of the item.
  • Valid cases will be resolved with a replacement, exchange, or refund at the shop's discretion — decided case by case.
  • Items without any issue are not returnable simply due to change of mind, unless the shop agrees otherwise.
Customer Conduct

Please provide a correct name, phone number, and address at checkout. Fake orders, abusive language, or repeated no-show at delivery may result in the shop refusing to serve future orders from that number.

Account, Login & Data Storage
  • Signing up or logging in on this site does not create a secured, password-protected account on a company server. There is no backup server — your name, phone, email, profile photo, cart, and order history are saved only inside your own browser on your own device.
  • If you clear your browser data, uninstall the browser, or switch to a different phone/browser, this information will be permanently lost and cannot be recovered by the shop, since nothing is stored centrally.
  • Moon Sky is not responsible for data lost this way. For anything important (like an Order ID), it's best to also save a screenshot or note it down separately.
  • Order and signup details relevant to fulfilling your order are separately sent to the shop's order inbox by email, and that copy is what the shop actually uses to process your order.
Reward Points
  • Points are earned as a thank-you for ordering (5 points per ₹100 spent) and can be used for a discount on a future order (1 point = ₹1).
  • Points have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for money, and are not a prepaid wallet or deposit of any kind.
  • Like the rest of your account data, your points balance is stored only on this device and is not backed up on a server. Clearing browser data will reset it to zero.
  • Moon Sky may change the earn rate, redemption value, or end the points program at any time.
Support / Contact

For any question, order issue, or complaint, message Moon Sky directly on WhatsApp using the chat button on this page, or use "Ask shop about my order" under My Orders. Keep your Order ID ready when you contact support.
